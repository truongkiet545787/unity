## Art direction

Target: stylized, readable, low-poly, bright tropical island. Prioritize clear racing readability over visual density. Large landmarks should help the player recognize zones: lighthouse/harbor, colorful village, beach palms, rocky canyon, glowing cave, cliff road above the sea.

## Legal/design constraint

Beach Buggy Racing and the supplied video are reference material only. Create original track geometry, original vehicle models or legally licensed assets, original UI, original logo and original environment dressing.
